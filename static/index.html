What am I looking at?

SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization, and Ices Explorer) is a NASA Medium Explorer mission mapping the entire sky in 102 near-infrared spectral channels from 0.75 to 5.0 micrometers.

Unlike traditional broadband astronomy cameras, SPHEREx uses Linear Variable Filters (LVFs) mounted directly above six detector arrays (D1 to D6). The observed wavelength continuously varies across the surface of each detector.

⚠️ Scientific Note on Temporal Changes:

Each frame shown here represents a different observation epoch as SPHEREx scanned the sky. Because the target landed on slightly different pixel coordinates across passes, each frame captures a slightly different slice of the infrared spectrum. Apparent brightness differences between frames mix true temporal variations with spectral spectral-energy differences.
